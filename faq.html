About Advanced TRS

Zeolite Detox Questions

Advanced TRS targets positively charged heavy metals and toxins — primarily mercury, lead, aluminium, arsenic, and cadmium, but also tin, barium, and various radioactive isotopes. The zeolite's selective negative charge is attracted to these heavy metal ions specifically. It does not strip beneficial macro-minerals like calcium, magnesium, or potassium from the body, as these are not absorbed or displaced by the zeolite cage structure under normal use.
Clinoptilolite zeolite has been granted GRAS (Generally Recognised As Safe) status by the FDA — meaning it has been reviewed and deemed safe for human consumption based on available scientific evidence and its history of use. Advanced TRS uses lab-grown clinoptilolite, which eliminates the contamination risk associated with mined zeolite. The product is third-party lab tested for purity before each batch is released. It is non-toxic, non-accumulative, and excreted completely from the body.
The standard protocol is 2 sprays in the morning and 3 sprays in the evening, directly into the mouth. Many customers report noticeable improvements in energy, mental clarity, and sleep within the first 2–4 weeks. However, heavy metal detoxification at the deep cellular level is a gradual process — most significant results are reported over 3–6 months of consistent daily use. The body has typically accumulated toxins over years or decades, and safe, thorough removal takes time.
Some people — particularly those with higher toxin loads — experience a mild adjustment period in the first 1–2 weeks. This can include fatigue, mild headaches, or changes in bowel habits, as the body begins mobilising stored toxins. This is generally a positive sign. To minimise this, it is recommended to drink plenty of filtered water (at least 2 litres per day) while using TRS to support the excretion pathways. If symptoms are significant, temporarily reducing to 1–2 sprays per day is advised before gradually increasing.
Advanced TRS is generally well-tolerated alongside most supplements. However, as a precaution, it is recommended to space TRS sprays at least 30 minutes away from oral medications or other supplements, as the zeolite could theoretically bind to some compounds before they are absorbed. Always consult your healthcare practitioner if you have a specific medical condition or are on prescription medication before starting any new supplement protocol.
